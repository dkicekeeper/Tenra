# Tenra

A native iOS app for personal finance: accounts, transactions, budgets, deposits, loans, recurring
payments, savings goals, debts and assets, with the assistant Ten. Twelve languages; Tenra Pro by
subscription or once.

Now: live in the App Store; growth work runs by [promotion-plan.md](promotion-plan.md) and
[plan-growth.md](plan-growth.md), product work by the plans in flight (`plan-*.md`) and the tasks
in [backlog/](backlog/).
