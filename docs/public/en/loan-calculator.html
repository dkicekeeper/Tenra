How a loan payment is counted

With equal payments you pay the same amount every month. At first most of it is interest, towards the end most of it is principal.

Payment = Amount × Rate / 12 / (1 − (1 + Rate / 12) ^ −Months)

With decreasing payments the principal is repaid in equal parts and interest is counted on what is left. The first payments are higher, but you pay less interest overall.

Instalment plans

An interest-free instalment plan is simple: the amount divided by the number of months. Set the rate to 0 and the calculator shows the payment.

Early repayment

Everything you pay above the schedule lowers the principal. Next month's interest is counted on a smaller balance, so even a small extra payment shortens the term and the interest.

Common questions

Which payment type costs less?

Decreasing payments cost less interest, but the first payments are higher. Equal payments are easier to budget: the amount is the same every month.

How do I pay less interest on a loan?

Pay early, above all in the first months, when the balance is largest. Enter the amount in "Extra payment every month" and the calculator shows the saving.

Does the calculator count fees and insurance?

No. It counts only the interest at the rate. Add fees and insurance to the interest yourself.
