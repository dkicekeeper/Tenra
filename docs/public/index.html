Tenra: all your money in one app

Private finance tracking on iPhone. Your data stays on the device, and Tenra never asks for a bank login.

Download on the App Store

iPhone, iOS 26 or later. Your data stays on the device, no bank login.

What Tenra does

  • Records Apple Pay payments by itself and takes an expense by voice or text: "coffee 4.50".
  • Keeps deposits: interest accrues every day and compounding is counted for you.
  • Keeps loans and instalment plans: the payment schedule, what is left to pay, early payments.
  • Shows where the money goes: categories, budgets, subscriptions, merchants.
  • Counts in several currencies at the real rate.
  • Reads bank statements in PDF and receipts from a photo.

Privacy

Tenra never asks for your bank login and never sees your accounts. Every record is kept on your phone, and the backup goes to your own iCloud.
