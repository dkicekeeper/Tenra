Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Tenra ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal finance app. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what stays on your device, what leaves it and why.

1. Data Storage

Your financial data (accounts, transactions, budgets, categories, deposits, loans, recurring payments, savings goals and personal debts) is stored on your device with Apple's Core Data framework. Backups are saved in the app's folder on your device or, if you turn iCloud backups on, in your own iCloud Drive, which Apple holds under your Apple Account. We cannot read your data or your backups.

2. Ten, the AI Assistant

Ten is optional. Before your first question goes to the cloud, the app asks for your consent; you can turn this off at any time in Settings › Assistant › Answers from the cloud. Simple records such as "coffee 1500" are drafted on your device without the cloud.

  • What is sent: your question (typed, or dictated and turned into text), and what Ten looks up on your device to answer it: totals, categories, account names, the transactions it needs, your savings goals and debts by name, and the facts you asked it to remember. For an attached receipt or bank statement, only a summary read on your device is sent (period, totals, categories), never the document.
  • Who processes it: the request goes through Tenra's server to Anthropic, which answers with its Claude model. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms: it does not use it to train models and keeps it only for a limited period for safety and abuse review.
  • What Tenra's server keeps: no questions and no answers. To count free answers it keeps an Apple App Attest key for your installation with a monthly number of answers (up to 40 days), and it checks with RevenueCat, by your anonymous app user ID, whether you have Tenra Pro (cached for 10 minutes).
  • What stays on your device: your database, your chats, your receipts and statements, and the facts Ten remembers, which you can see and delete in Settings › Assistant › What Ten knows about me.

3. Purchases

Tenra Pro is sold through the App Store; Apple handles payment. We use RevenueCat to know which purchases are active. RevenueCat receives your purchase history from Apple with an anonymous app user ID, not your name or email.

4. Other Network Requests

  • Currency rates: from the jsDelivr CDN (with a Cloudflare mirror) and the National Bank of Kazakhstan. These requests contain only currency codes and dates.
  • Logos: from Tenra's logo collection on jsDelivr, logo.dev and Google's favicon service. These requests contain only a company or service name or its website address.

None of these requests contain your financial data.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account: we do not ask for your name, email or phone number
  • No bank logins, bank account numbers or card details
  • No analytics or tracking services, and no advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • No location
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising

6. Device Permissions

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: for voice input. Speech is recognized by Apple's Speech framework, on your device when your language supports it; otherwise Apple processes the audio under its own privacy policy. For Ten, only the resulting text is sent, as described above.
  • Camera: to scan receipts. Images are read on your device.
  • Files: to import bank statements (CSV, PDF) you select. Files are read on your device.
  • Notifications: for local reminders and insights about your money, created on your device.

7. Data Security and Deletion

Your data is protected by iOS security: device encryption, app sandboxing and your passcode or biometrics. You can delete all your data at any time in the app's Settings; deleting the app also removes it from your device. Backups in iCloud Drive can be deleted from the app's backup screen.

8. Children's Privacy and Changes

Tenra is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. We may update this Privacy Policy; changes are shown by the "Last updated" date above and in app updates.
