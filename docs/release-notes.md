# Tenra, next version: What's New (ASC, 13 storefront locales and en-GB)

Version number not set yet (the owner's call). `prepare-version` (Actions → App Store Connect,
[asc.md](asc.md)) pastes each block into App Store Connect → the version → the locale → "What's
New in This Version"; en-GB (search in Kazakhstan) carries ru's text. No em dashes (user rule).
Names of screens as the app shows them (`settings.title`, `settings.spendingReminder.title`).

New since 1.8 (in the store since 2026-11-04):
- Ten's evening spending reminder ([plan-spending-reminder.md](plan-spending-reminder.md)):
  on days with nothing recorded, rarer after silence, a goodbye on day 10, no amounts; offered
  after the first entry on Home or in Ten's chat; Settings › Spending reminder; free.
- Amounts in the region's number format (decimal and grouping separators); Analytics on day
  one: the daily average of the period under way divides by the days so far, the health score
  waits for income, the top category and top merchant say their share in words.
- Settings › Formats ([plan-formats.md](plan-formats.md)): the date style, the first day of
  the week, separators, the currency symbol's place and cents, each the region's until chosen;
  free. The weekly summary comes on the chosen first day (Settings › Insight alerts names it); Ten's
  kept messages, notifications and the widget follow a change.
- First run: the account step's name hint; the categories step 3 per row with a check under
  each tile; the auto-capture rows with chevrons, the statement a toggle (newcomers only, not
  in What's New).

## en-US

```
What's new in Tenra:
• Ten now reminds you in the evening on days with nothing logged: a short, light note that never shows an amount. If you don’t drop by, Ten writes less often and then lets you be. Turn it on when Ten asks after your first expense, or in Settings › Spending reminder.
• Amounts and dates now follow your region: the order of day and month, separators, the currency symbol's place and the first day of the week. To use your own, open Settings › Formats.
• In Analytics, the daily average of the current month counts only the days so far, and Top Spending Category and Top Merchant state their share of spending in words.
```

## Submission checklist

1. **CI green**, then a TestFlight build from `main` (Actions → TestFlight, [asc.md](asc.md)).
2. **Device check**: Ten's offer after the first expense on Home and after a draft saved in
   Ten's chat; "Remind me" shows the system prompt; the push at the set time on a day with
   nothing recorded, none after an entry (a manual one, Ten's draft, an Apple Pay capture); a
   tap opens Ten; Settings › Spending reminder switches it and moves the time.
   First run on a fresh install: category names fit 3 per row, the checks toggle; amounts read
   "1 234,56 ₸" with a Russian or Kazakh region, "1,234.56" with a US one, "1.234,56 €" with a
   Spanish one (grouped below 10 000 too), "R$ 1.234,56" with a Brazilian one (the symbol first,
   with a space), on Home and in the widget.
   Settings › Formats: a choice changes the preview at once and leaves the page open; Home,
   History and Analytics show it after going back, without a relaunch; "Use the region's
   format" shows only after a change. VoiceOver reads amounts as numbers with "1’234.56" and
   "1.234,56". The widget shows amounts and dates in a chosen format. A system DatePicker (the
   add form) and the date range sheet start the week on the chosen day. The date range sheet's
   header ("3 нояб. 2026 – 10 нояб. 2026", longer in pt-BR and es) fits. The Home filter chip
   fits with "This week" and with a custom range. Settings › Backups dates each backup in the
   chosen style with its year, then the time. A change while Analytics has a page open (a
   category's breakdown, an insight) leaves the page open and shows the new format there; Ten's
   message on Home reads the new format; Settings › Insight alerts names the chosen first day.
   Refuse the system prompt from quiet (provisional) notifications and check iOS Settings: if
   Tenra stays provisional, reminders arrive quietly while the attribute says `denied`.
3. **The store build**: `prepare-version --version X --build N`.
4. **What's New**: this file (`prepare-version`), en-GB included.
5. **App Privacy** (founder, in App Store Connect; not in the API): the RevenueCat attribute
   `spending_reminder` (on, off, declined, denied) is Product Interaction, used for analytics,
   not linked to the user's identity, not for tracking. Update the answers before sending.
6. **One submission**: `asc.py prepare-review --version X`; the founder sends it.
