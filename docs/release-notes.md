# Tenra 1.5: What's New (ASC, 13 storefront locales)

Version 1.5, prepared 2026-10-12. Code: `main` from `a900aff9` on (CI green before the build).
Paste each block into App Store Connect → Tenra → 1.5 → the locale → "What's New in This Version".
No em dashes (user rule). Promotional Text stays as in 1.4. When 1.5 ships, move this file to
`archive/release-notes-1.5.md`.

New since 1.4 (submitted 2026-10-06):
- Hide amounts: the eye button on Home draws every amount as dots.
- Wallet merchants in Settings: the category and subcategory remembered per Apple Pay merchant, reassign or forget.
- Loans: this month's payment status, the unpaid total, payments from a card in another currency.
- Analytics: the category breakdown pages through periods; a subscription price increase shows for 30 days and is pushed once.
- Promo codes redeemed in Settings → Tenra Pro.
- Backups: consistent snapshots checked before listing, verified restore, automatic ones marked "Auto" and kept apart from manual ones, a failed automatic backup shows.
- DesignKit 3.4: livelier motion, loading skeletons, proportional digits in amounts, hero backgrounds in shades of one colour.
- Fixes: budgets read 0 on the Categories screen; currency equivalents and rates kept on edit, transfers and loan payments across currencies; a recurring payment doubled on its day; a time-zone change shifted transactions; detail screens missed an edit; projected balance per period; the database error screen instead of a silent fallback.

## en-US

```
What's new in Tenra 1.5:
• Hide your amounts with the eye button on Home, handy when you show the app to someone.
• Settings › Siri & Shortcuts › Wallet merchants: see the category Tenra remembered for each Apple Pay merchant, change it or forget the merchant.
• Loans show whether this month's payment is made and how much is left unpaid.
• Analytics: browse a category's breakdown period by period.
• Promo codes: redeem an App Store offer code in Settings.
• Sturdier backups: automatic ones are marked "Auto" and never replace yours, and a restore checks the backup before using it.
• Livelier animations and loading placeholders across the app.
• Fixes: budgets show your real spending, amounts in another currency keep their equivalent when you edit them, a recurring payment no longer appears twice on its day, and changing time zones no longer shifts your transactions.
```
